Temporal and disruption scenarios

Sensitivity-analysis assumptions only. They are not measured traffic, observed weather impacts or predictions from a trained model.

Hybrid model configuration

Model status: not trained. These controls document a proposed research configuration. No fitted LSTM, GRU or GNN is executed.

Research data readiness

Recommended data: timestamped GPS trajectories, road-segment IDs, observed travel-time labels, graph topology, road class, weather and incidents. Use chronological train/validation/test splits and simple baselines. Remove personal identifiers from GPS data and apply appropriate access controls.

Methodology, credits and limitations

Routing geometry and baseline duration are requested from a public OpenStreetMap-based routing service. Scenario duration is computed in this browser. No live traffic feed, local historical training dataset or trained neural network is connected. Route availability and quality depend on map coverage and public-service limits. Verify locally before operational use.
